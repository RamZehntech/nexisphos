01 — The 8 Weeks

What you'll actually do.

No tutorials, no toy projects. From week one you're shipping code that real clients see.

Onboarding & Foundations

Setup, Git workflow, code-review etiquette, the lab's design system, and a small starter task to get the muscles warm.

WEEK 03–05

Project One

Assigned to a real client engagement. You build features, get reviewed, ship to staging, then to production.

Final demo to the lab + clients. Top 20% get paid associate offers. Everyone leaves with a portfolio that gets interviews.

04 — Common Questions

Fellowship FAQ.

Why is there a fee? +
The fee covers your share of senior engineering time — every PR you write gets reviewed by a Lab Lead. It also signals seriousness; we don't want fellows who'll ghost in week three. The top 20% who join the associate team earn far more than the fee back in their first month.
Will I get a certificate? +
Yes — a verifiable certificate plus a personal recommendation letter from the Lab Lead. Both are LinkedIn-ready.
Is this remote or in-person? +
Hybrid. Saturdays in person at our Indore studio (mandatory — this is where the magic happens), weeknight syncs over video. If you're outside Indore, fully remote is possible for engineering fellows after week two.
What's the selection process? +
Step 1: WhatsApp application + portfolio link. Step 2: Small take-home task (2–3 hrs). Step 3: 30-min video chat with the Lab Lead. Step 4: Offer + ₹6,999 fee to confirm seat.
