07 — Common Questions

Things you're probably wondering.

Why do you call yourselves a research lab?
Because we treat every engagement as an experiment with a hypothesis, a measurable outcome, and a learning we carry into the next project. It's a methodology, not a marketing slogan. Our internal post-mortems and shared knowledge base are why we get faster and better with every client.
How long does a typical project take?
Probe: 10 days. Studio: 21 days. Bespoke: scoped per project, usually 4–8 weeks. We're aggressive on timelines because vague deadlines kill projects.
Are fellows building my project?
Fellows from the program may contribute under direct supervision of the lab lead. Every line of code is reviewed before deployment. This is how we keep prices honest without compromising quality. If you'd prefer senior-only delivery, choose the Bespoke tier.
What about hosting and domains?
We help you buy and set up your domain (you own it, always). For hosting, most sites run on Vercel or Hostinger — we'll recommend what fits your traffic and budget. Hosting cost is on you, usually ₹300–800/month.
Do you work with clients outside Indore?
Yes — across India and abroad. Most communication runs over WhatsApp, video calls, and Notion. The lab is just headquartered in Indore.
Can I see a contract before paying?
Always. Every engagement starts with a one-page scope agreement covering deliverables, timelines, payment terms, and IP transfer. 50% advance, 50% on launch. No fine print.
