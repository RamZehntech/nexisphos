Saachi Café.
3.2x table bookings in 60 days.

A 40-seat speciality café in Indore was running on a broken Wix site that took 8 seconds to load and converted nothing. We replaced it in 12 days with a custom site, integrated bookings, and a Google Business overhaul. Two months later they had to hire two more staff to handle the rush.

01 — The Brief

The problem.

Saachi Café had been open for 18 months. The food was great, the location was decent (a side lane off Vijay Nagar), but bookings were inconsistent and walk-ins were drying up as competition increased. Their existing Wix site was a relic — built by a friend's brother in 2024, never updated.

The site loaded in 8.3 seconds on mobile. The "Book a Table" button linked to a broken Google Form. Their Google Business Profile had 47 reviews but no photos uploaded since opening day. Their Instagram had 4,000 followers but the bio still pointed to the dead Wix site.

The owner, Rohan, came to us via a fellow founder's WhatsApp recommendation. His ask was simple: "Get me a website that actually works, fast, and helps me fill more tables."

02 — The Approach

Our hypothesis.

Most café websites optimise for the wrong thing — they show off interior photography and try to be art-directed Instagram pages. But the actual job of a café website is mundane: convince a hungry person on their phone, at 7pm, that this is where they should eat tonight, then make booking a table take less than 30 seconds.

We built around three bets:

(1) Speed beats beauty. A 1-second-load site with average photos beats a 6-second site with magazine-quality photos. Every time.

(2) Bookings need to be one tap. WhatsApp click-to-chat for "Book a table" — no forms, no app downloads, just an instant conversation in a tool 600M Indians already have open.

(3) Google Business is where the real traffic comes from. Most cafés ignore it. We treated it as the homepage.

03 — What We Built

The build.

A 6-page Next.js site running on Vercel with Sanity as the CMS. Custom design system in Saachi's brand colours (warm cream + deep green). Full responsive build tested on 8 devices. Page load: 0.9 seconds on 4G, 0.4 on Wi-Fi.

WhatsApp integration as the primary booking flow — one tap from any page opens a pre-filled message with the customer's selected date and party size. The owner's phone gets the lead instantly. No forms, no databases, no chasing emails.

Google Business Profile completely overhauled — proper categories, opening hours, menu items as products, weekly photo uploads scheduled, response templates for reviews. We also built schema markup so the café shows up in rich-snippet results when people search "best cafés in Vijay Nagar."

SEO foundation laid for 12 keywords spanning "cafés in Indore," "speciality coffee Indore," and long-tail dish queries like "best continental breakfast Vijay Nagar."

05 — What We Learned

For the next café.

Three lessons we're carrying into every F&B engagement after this one:

WhatsApp-first booking dominates form-first booking — by something like 4:1. Customers will tap a chat button who would never fill a form. Build for the human, not the data model.

Google Business reviews compound faster than you'd expect when you actively prompt happy customers post-meal. The QR code on the bill changed everything.

Speed is the most underrated lever in local SEO. Going from 8s → 0.9s didn't just lower bounce — it directly correlated with Google ranking jumps for half a dozen keywords within 30 days.
